# jobcairn.github.io

The website for [JobCairn](https://jobcairn.github.io), a local-first job search app for macOS built around Claude. It finds jobs, scores your fit, flags visa sponsorship wording and tailors your resume, and it keeps your whole search in one folder on your computer.

## What JobCairn does

- **Find jobs** in your own Chrome on SEEK, LinkedIn, Indeed and company career pages, read-only.
- **Score your fit** with interview odds and the evidence for each must-have.
- **Flag visa sponsorship** wording in each ad, and set aside ads that need work rights you don't have.
- **Tailor your resume and cover letter** from what's already in your resume and profile. Export to PDF or Word.
- **Track applications** and file job email from Gmail under the right job. Nothing is sent without your OK.
- **Prepare for interviews** with study guides, interview prep and short narrated videos.
